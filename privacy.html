Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026 · Stance Check

Stance Check ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile drill training app for wrestlers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.

The following limited data is collected automatically:

  • Anonymous user ID — a randomly generated identifier stored locally on your device and shared with RevenueCat to track your purchase status. This ID is not linked to your identity.
  • Purchase history — if you purchase Stance Check Pro, transaction data (product ID, purchase date, entitlement status) is processed by RevenueCat and Apple/Google to verify your purchase.
  • Device identifiers — standard identifiers used by the app store platforms (Apple App Store, Google Play) as part of their in-app purchase infrastructure.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To verify and restore your Stance Check Pro purchase across devices.
  • To unlock premium features when your entitlement is confirmed.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the app's core functionality.

3. Speech Synthesis

Stance Check uses your device's built-in speech synthesis engine to call out drill cues. This feature runs entirely on-device — no audio is recorded, transmitted, or stored.

4. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — we use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and entitlement status. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user ID and purchase data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple App Store / Google Play — in-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Their privacy policies govern how payment and transaction data are handled.

5. Data Retention

Your anonymous user ID is stored on your device and persists until you delete the app. RevenueCat retains purchase records in accordance with their own data retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Stance Check is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information tied to your identity, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our end. To reset your anonymous user ID, delete and reinstall the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
